Spotui Music Player is an expressive Jetpack Compose audio player built on Media3 with Spotify and YouTube Music playlist synchronization, multi-source synced lyrics, lossless mirror routing, 7-band Biquad EQ, and 3D binaural soundstage processing.

Features:

- Unified Spotify, YouTube Music, and Deezer library sync
- Background Media3 playback with Android Auto & Glance home screen widgets
- Offline downloads with MediaStore export into Music/spotui
- 7-Band Biquad EQ, Preamp, Bass Boost, RMS Normalizer & 3D Binaural Orbit DSP
- Independent 0-100% In-App Volume slider preserving OS media volume
- Resizable Accessibility Floating Mini-Player with Studio tabs & Game-only live teammate translation
- LRCLIB, BetterLyrics, SimpMusic, Paxsenix & KuGou synced lyrics with on-device translation
- Last.fm scrobbling & ShazamKit audio recognition telemetry
- Encrypted NovaAc v8 playlist & library archive backups
- Material 3 Expressive themes, dark/AMOLED surfaces & adaptive layouts
